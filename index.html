Dream Lab by The Kahaaniya is a 4-week guided journey where children discover who they are — through stories, nature, baking, creating, and sharing with the world.

The Journey

4 Weeks.
One Transformation.

Week 1
🌱

Story & Self Discovery

Every child has a story. We help them find it — through listening, sharing, and imagination.

Week 2
🌿

Nature & Responsibility

Children engage with the living world — plants, growth, care — and learn what it means to nurture.

Week 3
🎨

Create Something Real

Baking, making, building — children produce something tangible they can be proud of.

Week 4
🎤

Confidence Showcase

Each child presents their creation to a real audience. The world sees what they made.
